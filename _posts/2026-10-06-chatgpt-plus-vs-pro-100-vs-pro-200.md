ChatGPT Plus, Pro $100 and Pro $200 share the same core features. **What changes is the usage allowance**, so the right plan depends on how much you actually use it.

## The plans side by side

| Plan | Official price | Usage vs Plus | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plus | $20/mo | 1x | Everyday questions, writing, research, light coding |
| Pro $100 | $100/mo | 5x | Heavy daily use, regularly hitting limits |
| Pro $200 | $200/mo | 20x | All-day power use and parallel tasks |

Both Pro tiers have the same features. Going from $100 to $200 buys a bigger allowance, not new capabilities.

## When to upgrade

- **Start with Plus.** Most people never use up its allowance.
- **Move to Pro $100** if you hit usage limits several times a week and it slows your work.
- **Coding with Codex or running long deep-research tasks** burns allowance fastest, and is the most common reason to upgrade.
- **Pro $200** only makes sense if $100 still runs out regularly.
