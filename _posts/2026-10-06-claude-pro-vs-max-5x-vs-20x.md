Claude's three personal plans are nearly identical in features. They differ in **usage**, and both Pro and Max include Claude Code.

## Comparison

| Plan | Official price | Usage | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro | $20/mo ($17 if billed yearly) | Baseline | Chat, writing, occasional Claude Code |
| Max 5x | $100/mo | 5x Pro | Daily coding, hitting Pro limits |
| Max 20x | $200/mo | 20x Pro | All-day use or several Claude Code sessions at once |

Max 5x and Max 20x have the same features, only a different allowance.

## What "5x" and "20x" mean

Usage is measured in **rolling five-hour windows**, and the multiplier applies to each window. On top of that sits a **weekly cap**. So 20x is not 20 times everything, and heavy users can run into either limit.

## How to choose

- **Small projects, occasional coding:** Pro is enough.
- **Hitting the limit in a normal workday:** go to Max 5x.
- **Coding for hours every day, running parallel sessions, or hitting the weekly cap on 5x:** consider Max 20x.

If you only need Claude for building software through the API, prepaid API credit can fit better: you pay for what you use, with no monthly fee.
