ChatGPT, Claude and Cursor only take cards from supported countries. If yours is not one of them, there are four common routes, each with trade-offs, and none is best for everyone.

## The four options

| Option | How it works | Upside | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. App Store gift card | Load a US App Store balance, then subscribe inside the iPhone app | Official route, your own account | Needs a US Apple ID and an iPhone; in-app prices can differ from the website; only plans offered in the app |
| 2. Virtual card | Get a virtual card that works abroad and pay on the official site | Your own account, official price | Fees and identity checks; some issuers get declined; rules change often |
| 3. Top-up service | A third party pays and activates the plan on your own account | Your own account, often below list price | May need your login or access; choose a reputable seller |
| 4. Ready-made private account | Buy an account that is already subscribed and log in | Easiest, often the cheapest | The account was not registered by you, so judge for yourself whether it fits the provider's terms; pick a seller with real after-sales |

## Which to pick

- **Account safety first, and you do not mind the effort:** option 1 or 2.
- **Cheap and simple, for ordinary work:** option 4.
- **Attached to an account you already use:** option 3, from a trusted seller, without handing over password-reset control.

## What to check when buying a ready-made account

1. **Is it truly private?** Shared accounts give no guarantee on usage or stability.
2. **Is the after-sales policy explicit?** What happens if the plan stops partway, and is it compensated per day?
3. **Can you change the password and recovery email?** That is what real control of the account means.
4. **How fast is delivery?** Be wary of long, vague waits.
